About

Istanbul · TR / FA / AZ / EN

I'm a mechatronics engineer who spends his days with machines that want to fall over. At Acrome Robotics I test and support the rigs universities and research labs use to learn control: inverted pendulums, Ball & Beam systems, a Ball Balancing Table, Stewart platforms and delta robots.

I sit between the hardware and the people using it, taking teams from a Simulink model to a rig that actually balances.

Right now I'm going deeper into control theory and PID tuning, learning ROS 2, and exploring where computer vision and CNNs fit into robotics.
